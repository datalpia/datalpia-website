Sound familiar?

A prototype is easy. Running it in production, on your terms, is not.

  1. A

    No industrialisation strategy

    “It works in the notebook. Nobody knows how it reaches production.”

    Every model ships differently, or doesn’t ship. Delivery depends on whoever is available.

  2. B

    No shared MLOps philosophy

    “Which version is live? Who approved it? When do we retrain?”

    The model lifecycle is not defined: no stages, no owners, no rules for promotion or retirement.

  3. C

    Teams working in silos

    “Data science throws it over the wall. Engineering rewrites it.”

    Data scientists, software engineers and DevOps use different tools and vocabulary, and hand work off late.

  4. D

    AI used without a plan

    “We plugged an LLM into everything. Cost, data exposure and reliability are anyone’s guess.”

    Generic agents get used where plain rules or a small specialised model would be cheaper, faster and fully under your control.
